FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

What's included in the free first week?
Full access to all gym equipment, group classes, and the recovery zone for 7 consecutive days. No payment required, no card on file. Just walk in and start training.
Do I need to be fit before joining?
Not at all. We have members at every level — from complete beginners to competitive athletes. Your certified coach will design a program that matches your current fitness and builds from there.
Is there a lock-in contract?
No. All our plans are month-to-month (or quarterly/annual for better rates). You can cancel anytime with 7 days notice — no penalties, no hassle.
What are the gym timings?
We're open Monday to Saturday, 5:30 AM – 11:00 PM, and Sundays 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM. Coaches are available for personal training during all operating hours.
